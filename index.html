Arma 3 Preset Merger

Your group updates its base modpack, and you have to add all your client-side mods again. Upload your presets here to pull out your client-side mods and put them on top of the new base modpack.

Everything runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.

1 Find your client-side mods

Export both presets from the Arma 3 Launcher (MODS → PRESET → select preset → EXPORT). If your preset already contains only your client-side mods, leave the base modpack empty.

2 Apply them to a new base modpack

Upload the new base modpack to get a preset with the new base plus your client-side mods.
